PHISHING INDICATORS ANALYSIS REPORT

Sample 01 – Email Investigation

1. Email Information

- Sender: attack@attacker.example.com
- Subject: Monday, March 7 2022
- Date: 7 March 2022
- Displayed Brand: MetroFax / SharePoint
- Return-Path: attack@attacker.example.com
- Recipient: Undisclosed recipients
- Suspicious URL: https://attacker.example.com/

2. Identified Phishing Indicators

Indicator 1: Suspicious Sender Domain

The sender address does not match the displayed MetroFax brand.

Indicator 2: Brand Impersonation

The email uses SharePoint and MetroFax branding to appear legitimate.

Indicator 3: Suspicious Hyperlink

The Print & Preview Here link points to attacker.example.com instead of an official MetroFax domain.

Indicator 4: Unexpected Attachment

The email claims that a PDF fax is available, which may encourage users to open an unexpected file. No actual attachment was included in the supplied sample.

Indicator 5: Hidden Recipients

The email is addressed to undisclosed recipients.

Indicator 6: Unrelated Footer

The NHSmail information in the footer is unrelated to the MetroFax fax notification.

3. Email Authentication Results

- SPF: Not available
- DKIM: Not available
- DMARC: Not available

Note: Complete authentication headers were not provided, so authentication results cannot be verified.

4. Risk Classification

Classification: PHISHING

Risk Level: HIGH

Reason: Multiple suspicious indicators were identified, including sender mismatch, brand impersonation and a suspicious hyperlink.

5. Recommended Security Actions

1. Do not click suspicious email links.
2. Do not open unexpected attachments.
3. Verify the sender through official channels.
4. Report suspicious emails to the IT security team.
5. Follow organizational email security policies.

6. Analyst Conclusion

The analyzed email uses a fake fax notification theme to encourage recipients to visit a suspicious link. Multiple phishing indicators were identified. Therefore, the email is classified as HIGH RISK for this educational analysis.

Note: The example.com domain is reserved for documentation and educational purposes. This sample does not establish a real malicious domain.
