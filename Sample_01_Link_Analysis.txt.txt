Sample 01 – Suspicious URL Analysis

Email Subject: Monday, March 7 2022

Displayed Link Text: Print & Preview Here

Extracted URL: https://attacker.example.com/

Protocol: HTTPS

Domain: attacker.example.com

Findings

1. The URL domain does not match the displayed MetroFax brand.
2. The link is presented as a fax preview action.
3. The sender domain and link domain are the same.
4. The URL uses HTTPS, but HTTPS alone does not guarantee that a website is legitimate.
5. The domain example.com is reserved for documentation and educational examples.

Risk Assessment

Classification: Suspicious – Educational Phishing Sample

Risk Level: High (based on the email's phishing indicators).

Recommendation

- Do not click suspicious email links.
- Verify the sender using official communication channels.
- Check the actual destination URL before trusting a link.
- Report suspicious emails to the security team.
