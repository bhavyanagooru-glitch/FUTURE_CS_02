SAMPLE 2 – HTML ATTACHMENT AND LINK ANALYSIS

Email Type: Fake Voicemail Notification

Attachment Name:
audio_john.doe@target.example.com_file.htm

Attachment Type: HTML

Observed Redirect Domain:
attacker.example.com

Findings:

1. Email asks the recipient to download an HTML attachment.
2. The attachment was associated with an Outlook-style sign-in page.
3. The supplied HTML sample contains a browser redirect.
4. The page may imitate a legitimate login page.
5. The recipient could be tricked into sharing account credentials.

Potential Impact:
Credential theft and redirection to an untrusted website.

Recommendation:
Do not open unexpected HTML attachments.
Never enter passwords through links in unexpected emails.

Classification: High Risk – Phishing

Note:
The domain is anonymized. The attachment was not executed.
