EMPLOYEE PHISHING AWARENESS GUIDE

How to Identify Phishing Emails

1. Check the sender's email address carefully.
2. Be cautious of urgent or threatening messages.
3. Do not click unknown or suspicious links.
4. Never open unexpected email attachments.
5. Check spelling, grammar and unusual formatting.
6. Verify requests through official communication channels.
7. Never share passwords or OTPs through email links.

What to Do When You Receive a Suspicious Email

1. Do not reply to the email.
2. Do not click links or download attachments.
3. Report the email to the IT or Security Team.
4. Follow your organization's security procedures.
5. Delete the email only according to organizational policy.

Security Best Practices

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Keep systems and antivirus software updated.
- Participate in security awareness training.
- Always verify unexpected requests for sensitive information.

Conclusion

Employee awareness is an important part of cybersecurity. Identifying suspicious emails and reporting them promptly can help prevent phishing attacks and protect organizational data.
